# Governance Metrics Framework

## Purpose

This framework defines the metrics used to measure security-governance performance.

---

## Metrics

### Patch Compliance

Definition:

Percentage of systems within patching policy requirements.

Target:

95%

Thresholds:

- Green: ≥95%
- Amber: 90-94%
- Red: <90%

---

### Vulnerability Remediation Time

Definition:

Average number of days required to remediate vulnerabilities.

Target:

7 days

---

### Security Incidents

Definition:

Number of security incidents recorded during the reporting period.

Target:

1 or fewer incidents

---

### Monitoring Coverage

Definition:

Percentage of systems actively monitored.

Target:

100%

---

### Policy Compliance

Definition:

Percentage of implemented governance controls meeting policy requirements.

Target:

95%

---

## Reporting Frequency

- Weekly Operational Reporting
- Monthly Management Review
- Quarterly Board Reporting
